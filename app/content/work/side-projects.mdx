A rotating cast of small tools and experiments:

- **CLI utilities** — small developer tools, mostly Rust and
  TypeScript, built to remove paper cuts from my own workflow.
- **Homelab** — self-hosted services, infrastructure-as-code for a
  closet datacenter that is definitely not over-engineered.
- **Experiments** — graphics toys, generative art, and whatever
  rabbit hole this month provides. The CRT shader on this site
  started as one of these.

Find the public ones on GitHub.
